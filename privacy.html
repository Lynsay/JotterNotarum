Jotter Notarum: Feedback Generator - Privacy

Your information stays on your computer

Jotter Notarum runs entirely in your web browser. Your comment file, the comments you select, the feedback you generate and any comments you add are not sent to a server, to me, or to anyone else.

What the tool does with your information

  • Your comment file is read by your browser, on your computer. It is never uploaded.
  • Generated feedback lives only in the text box on the page. It is placed on your clipboard only when you press "Copy Feedback". Reloading or closing the page clears it.
  • Comments you add are written back to your own .txt file on your computer. In Chrome and Edge your browser asks your permission first. In other browsers the tool offers an updated copy to download.
  • Word files and class lists (on the Advanced page) are handled entirely on your computer. A Word file is created in your browser and saved to a place you choose, or added to a Word file you choose. A class list is read in your browser and kept in memory only: student names are not stored, not sent anywhere, and are forgotten when you close or reload the page.
  • Your light/dark mode choice is the only thing the tool remembers. It is stored as a small setting called "theme" in your browser's local storage, on your device, and is never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

What the tool does not do

  • No accounts or logins.
  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts or other outside resources. Everything the pages need is served with the site itself.

Student information

Nothing you enter is uploaded, and using the tool does not in itself share anyone's data. Feedback you copy out of it and paste into another system is covered by that system's rules and by your institution's policies. Keeping names and other personal details out of your comment bank is a good habit.

Check for yourself

If you open your browser's developer tools and look at the Network tab, you will see that no requests are made while you use the tool once the page has loaded.
