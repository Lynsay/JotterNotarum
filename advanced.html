Jotter Notarum: Feedback Generator - Advanced

1Select your feedback comments file

This must be a .txt file formatted according to the instructions.

2Select your feedback phrases

    3Generate feedback text

    Click the button below to generate feedback text. You can also add your own text to the generated comments.

    4Copy feedback and clear page

    Copy the feedback and paste it into My Learning Space. Alternatively, reset the page to its original state.


    5Export to Word (optional)

    Save the feedback in the text box above as a Word document on your computer. Nothing is uploaded. Each student's feedback starts with their name in bold.
